# Bushisa Data Retention Policy

## Scope

This policy covers retention and deletion rules for Bushisa platform data, including user accounts, profiles, messages, confessions, swipes, matches, reports, audit logs, and consent records.

## Active Accounts

Data for active accounts is retained for the duration of account activity so that Bushisa can provide matchmaking, messaging, confessions, safety, and account management features.

Accounts with no login activity for 12 months are flagged for review. Review may result in user notification, account restriction, anonymisation, or deletion depending on platform policy and applicable legal requirements.

## Deleted Accounts

When a user deletes their account, user data is anonymised within 30 days.

Profile data is wiped, including display name, biography, profile photo references, preferences, and other identifying profile details. Confession authorship is set to anonymous where the database schema supports anonymous attribution.

Audit logs that reference the deleted user are retained for 12 months for legal compliance, safety investigations, and abuse prevention. After 12 months, those audit logs are purged unless a legal hold or active investigation requires longer retention.

## Messages

Messages are retained for the lifetime of the match so users can access their conversation history.

If both users in a match delete their accounts, the related messages are permanently deleted.

## Reports & Moderation Logs

Reports and moderation logs are retained for 24 months after resolution. This retention period supports compliance, appeals, repeat-abuse detection, and safety reviews.

## Audit Logs

Audit logs are retained for 12 months, then automatically purged unless they are required for an active investigation, legal request, or compliance review.

## Consent Records

Consent records are retained indefinitely as a legal requirement to prove that consent was obtained, including consent for terms of service, privacy policy, data processing, and related platform requirements.

## Backups

Database backups are retained for 30 days. Backup deletion follows the normal backup rotation schedule.

## Legal Basis

This policy is based on the Zimbabwe Data Protection Act [Chapter 11:12] and POPIA for cross-border users from South Africa.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually.
